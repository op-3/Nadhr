# Licence clarification requests: CANCELLED

> **Cancelled on 2026-11-27 by owner decision.** Nadhr does not contact feed
> providers, request permissions, negotiate licences, or buy threat
> intelligence. Only sources whose published terms already allow the intended
> use are used; anything ambiguous is excluded. The drafts below were never
> sent and are kept only as a historical record. Do not act on them.


These drafts ask rights holders for permission or clarification. **None has
been sent.** They go out in the owner's name, so each one needs the owner's
approval and a confirmed official contact channel first. Record any replies
here, together with the decision taken.

---

## 1. abuse.ch / Spamhaus: URLhaus and ThreatFox

**Channel:** the official contact listed at https://abuse.ch/ (to be confirmed
before sending).

> Subject: Licensing question: URLhaus data in an open-source web browser
>
> Hello,
>
> We develop Nadhr, a free, open-source (GPL-3.0) web browser based on
> Chromium. We would like to protect users from malware sites by matching
> navigations locally against URLhaus host data. No browsing data would be sent
> to us or to anyone else.
>
> Your Terms of Use (21 October 2026) allow not-for-profit use and say that
> commercial use may require a subscription. Could you clarify:
>
> 1. Is it permitted for a free, open-source browser to include URLhaus-derived
>    host lists, and to publish them through a signed update mirror
>    (redistribution)?
> 2. Would that remain permitted if the browser is used inside companies or
>    government agencies, or if paid enterprise support for it is offered
>    later? If not, what would the terms be?
> 3. What attribution do you require?
>
> We will not use the data by default until we have your answer.
>
> Thank you for running URLhaus.

## 2. PhishStats

>

>

> Thank you.
